# AI safety and governance notes

## Approved data sources
- Mock/sample survey exports only.
- No live customer data used in this project.

## Data handling
- No personal identifying information is enriched or inferred.
- All comments are treated as anonymized survey text.

## Human review triggers
- Safety concerns (injuries, hazards)
- Harassment, discrimination, or conduct issues
- Privacy or legal risk
- Low-confidence or ambiguous classification
- Multiple conflicting themes in one comment

## Output labeling
- All AI-generated summaries are labeled as draft outputs.
- Draft outputs require manager or reviewer sign-off before action is taken.
